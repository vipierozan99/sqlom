--- cost of the in-transaction guard on the read path ---
engine.fetch_all() checks a contextvar so that calling it inside
engine.transaction() raises instead of silently using another connection.
Microbenchmark: ContextVar.get() 54 ns; whole guard 66 ns = 0.015% of a
0.43 ms request. Paired A/B (one process, alternating rounds, median of 5,
psycopg pool 10, c=8, core 0):

  with guard      1809 rps   spread 17.3%
  no guard        1811 rps   spread  8.6%
  -> guard costs +0.11% (+2 rps), i.e. inside the noise
